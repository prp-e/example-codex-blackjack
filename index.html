♠ The Velvet Room
How to play

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Hit to draw a card, or stand to keep your total. Aces count as 1 or 11. The dealer uses a six-deck shoe and reshuffles between hands when fewer than 60 cards remain. The dealer stands on all 17s. An ace and a ten-value card on the opening deal make blackjack.
